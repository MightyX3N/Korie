Korie

Add-ons bring other services (DRTV, Stremio, RomM ...) into Jellyfin: each one you turn on shows as a channel. Korie also reads your libraries the way your folders are laid out: groups of shows, films split in parts (CD 1, CD 2) and your own .nfo files.

Repositories

Places Korie gets add-ons from, one address per line. Each holds an index.json in the Korie Store's format. Only add repositories you trust: their add-ons run on this server.
